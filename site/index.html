Play chess against yourself.

It learns how you play from your own games, then sits across the board as you. Everything stays on your computer. No account, no server, nothing uploaded.

Windows .exe installer macOS, Apple silicon .dmg macOS, Intel .dmg Linux .tar.gz

The first launch downloads the model and a move checker (about 120 MB) and checks them against fixed fingerprints. Then tell it your Chess.com or Lichess name, or give it a PGN file.

Your system will warn you once

  • Windows: "Windows protected your PC" → More info → Run anyway.
  • macOS: open it once, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

The app isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. To check a download, compare it with SHA256SUMS on the release page.
